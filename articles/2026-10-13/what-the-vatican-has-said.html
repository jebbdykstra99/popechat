What the Vatican Has Said: Pope Leo XIV's Lung Surgery, 11–19 October 2026

Teaser: Pope Leo XIV, Gemelli: Vatican News https://www.vaticannews.va/en/pope/news/2026-10/pope-leo-surgery-gemelli-hospital-successful.html Holy See bulletin: "The operation was successful" https://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/en/bollettino/pubblico/2026/10/13/261013b.html

popechat.com is an independent Catholic community site. It is not an official Vatican outlet. This page quotes only the Holy See Press Office and Vatican News, and every quotation was checked against the live page on 19 October 2026. All dates are Rome dates.

FACT: The official timeline

Sunday 11 October: the Pope's own announcement

After the Angelus, Pope Leo XIV told the faithful in St Peter's Square: "Dear friends, during some recent medical examinations, the doctors found a small, localized nodule in my lung. It was detected at a very early stage and is well-localized. The doctors believe that it is best to intervene, so, following their advice, I have decided to undergo surgery to remove it. The operation is already scheduled to take place in the coming days. I thank you for your spiritual closeness and entrust myself to your prayers. Thank you, and happy Sunday!" (Vatican News)

Answering journalists, Press Office Director Matteo Bruni said "the operation is scheduled for the first days of this week and the estimated post-operative recovery time will be about one week." (Vatican News)

Monday 12 October: audiences cancelled

The Prefecture of the Papal Household announced: "Due to the Holy Father's hospitalization, all audiences have been cancelled until Tuesday 20 October." (Holy See Press Office)

Vatican News reported that Bruni "clarified that the examinations had been routine checks," and that the Pope "held all his scheduled audiences on Monday morning." (Vatican News)

Tuesday 13 October: admission

The Press Office told journalists: "As announced, Pope Leo XIV has been admitted to the Agostino Gemelli University Hospital. This afternoon, he will undergo surgery for a nodule in the lung, performed by the thoracic surgery team led by Professor Stefano Margaritora." (Holy See Press Office)

Vatican News described the surgery as one to remove a "small, localized nodule" from his lung. It reported that the Pope "arrived at Gemelli Hospital at 1:07 p.m." and that Bruni "confirmed that Pope Leo is expected to remain in the hospital for about a week after surgery." (Vatican News)

Tuesday 13 October, evening: the medical bulletin

The Press Office published a statement from Dr Luigi Carbone and Professor Stefano Margaritora (Holy See Press Office):

"The Holy Father underwent thoracic surgery as scheduled."

"To ensure a radical procedure, the lower lobe of the right lung was removed using a video-assisted technique. The intraoperative histological examination confirmed the diagnosis of lung adenocarcinoma, as suspected by the pre-operative tests."

"The Holy Father was in the operating theatre for approximately three hours; the operation itself lasted about ninety minutes. The operation was successful and there were no complications."

"Following the operation, the Pope woke up normally and, after a brief period of observation, returned to his room on the tenth floor."

The bulletin itself refers to "The Holy Father" and does not name the hospital; the Gemelli and the Pope's name come from the Press Office note of 13 October and Vatican News, cited above.

The bulletin adds that "The surgical team was led by Professor Stefano Margaritora." (Holy See Press Office)

Vatican News added that "Following the operation, Pope Leo asked for dinner and spent the evening in the company of his secretaries and other Vatican staff, according to the Director of the Holy See Press Office, Matteo Bruni." (Vatican News)

The same report notes: "In the coming days, post-operation exams will be conducted on the removed tissue." It also says "No change has been communicated regarding the previously announced hospitalisation time of one week." That report said the Press Office would give an update on Wednesday morning, and it did (see below). (Vatican News)

Wednesday 14 October, morning: the night

Vatican News reported that the Holy See Press Office said on Wednesday: "The night passed calmly for the Pope. Further information will be provided later today." (Vatican News)

The same report notes: "No change has been communicated regarding the previously announced hospitalization time of one week." (Vatican News)

Wednesday 14 October, afternoon: the doctors' statement

Vatican News reported that the Press Office released a statement from Dr Luigi Carbone and Professor Stefano Margaritora on Pope Leo XIV's postoperative recovery (Vatican News):

"This morning, the Holy Father underwent the blood tests and chest X-ray scheduled after surgery. The results confirm that his postoperative recovery is progressing normally: the values are within the expected range for the type of operation performed."

"The Holy Father has undergone respiratory physiotherapy and has begun walking around his room. He has resumed eating normally and had breakfast and lunch."

"His recovery is proceeding as expected."

Vatican News also reported that Matteo Bruni later told journalists the Pope concelebrated Mass on Wednesday afternoon "in a room specially prepared for the purpose on the 10th floor of Gemelli Hospital." (Vatican News)

Thursday 15 October, morning

Vatican News reported: "The Pope had a good night in the hospital, got up and had breakfast," the Holy See Press Office said on Thursday morning. (Vatican News)

The same report notes: "Since the surgery took place, no changes have been communicated regarding the previously announced hospitalization time of one week." (Vatican News)

Thursday 15 October, afternoon: the doctors' statement

Vatican News published the following update from Dr Luigi Carbone and Professor Stefano Margaritora on the Pope's postoperative recovery (Vatican News):

"This morning, the Holy Father underwent routine checks, which yielded normal results. The chest X-ray showed good recovery of the lung that underwent surgery. He carried out his daily activities independently. His physiotherapy session included a full range of physical activities."

"Overall, his postoperative recovery is progressing normally."

Thursday 15 October: Mass, work and a meeting (Press Office)

Vatican News attributed the following to the Holy See Press Office (Vatican News):

The Holy See Press Office reported that the Pope concelebrated Mass on Thursday morning in a room specially prepared for the purpose on the 10th floor of Gemelli Hospital, as he had done on Wednesday afternoon.

As he resumed his work, Pope Leo met on Thursday afternoon with the Substitute for General Affairs, Archbishop Paolo Rudelli, according to the Holy See Press Office.

Friday 16 October: the doctors' statement

Vatican News reported that the Holy See Press Office released a statement from Dr Luigi Carbone and Professor Stefano Margaritora (Vatican News). The doctors said the Pope "underwent blood tests and a chest X-ray this morning, which confirmed the steady improvement in his overall condition." They added that "respiratory physiotherapy sessions have continued," noting that his "postoperative recovery allows him to carry out normal daily activities entirely independently." (Vatican News)

Friday 16 October: Mass and Sunday's Angelus (Press Office)

Vatican News reported that the Holy See Press Office said separately that Pope Leo XIV celebrated Mass at noon in the room set up on the 10th floor of Gemelli Hospital, and that he distributed Communion. Several members of Gemelli's medical and nursing staff attended, along with members of the Vatican City State Gendarmerie Corps. (Vatican News)

The Press Office statement read: "In his homily, the Pope thanked all the staff—doctors, nurses, and orderlies—for the work they do every day caring for patients in their frailty, and especially for what they have done and continue to do for him." (Vatican News)

The Holy See Press Office said the Sunday noon-day Angelus "will take place." (Vatican News)

Friday 16 October, afternoon: meetings at Gemelli (Press Office)

Vatican News reported (article updated 5:25 PM Rome / dateModified 2026-10-16T17:27:41+02:00) that, according to the Holy See Press Office, Pope Leo XIV met separately on Friday afternoon at Gemelli Hospital with Cardinal Luis Antonio Tagle, Pro-Prefect of the Dicastery for Evangelization and Dean of the College of Cardinals, and Cardinal Baldo Reina, Vicar General for the Diocese of Rome. (Vatican News)

Saturday 17 October, morning: fit for discharge (doctors' statement)

The Holy See Press Office published a statement from Dr Luigi Carbone and Professor Stefano Margaritora regarding the Pope's post-operative recovery: "In light of the blood tests and chest X-ray carried out this morning, and following an evaluation of his clinical condition, the Holy Father has been deemed fit for discharge." (Holy See Press Office)

Saturday 17 October: return to the Vatican and Sunday's Angelus (Press Office)

The Press Office said: "Following the doctors' conclusions this morning, Pope Leo XIV will be discharged today from the Gemelli Hospital, where he was admitted for surgery on his right lung. He will leave the hospital in the early afternoon and return to the Vatican. Tomorrow he will pray the Angelus from the window of the papal apartment." (Holy See Press Office)

Vatican News reported that, according to the Press Office, before returning to the Vatican the Pope would celebrate Mass in the room set aside for that purpose on the 10th floor of Gemelli Hospital. (Vatican News)

Vatican News reported that afternoon that Pope Leo XIV "has been discharged from Gemelli Hospital and has returned to the papal apartments in Vatican City State, where he will lead the Angelus on Sunday." (Vatican News)

The Press Office later said, in Italian, that Pope Leo XIV left the Gemelli hospital that afternoon and returned to the Vatican. On leaving, it said, he thanked Professor Stefano Margaritora, who performed the operation, together with the medical and nursing team, and greeted the healthcare staff, Professor Elena Beccalli, Rector of the Catholic University of the Sacred Heart, and the hospital's leadership. It added that on Sunday 18 October he will recite the Angelus from the Apostolic Palace. (Our translation; Holy See Press Office)

Saturday 17 October: the surgeon's interview (Vatican News)

In an interview with Vatican News, Professor Margaritora said: "At the time of his discharge from the hospital, Pope Leo was in entirely satisfactory clinical condition. His blood test results were completely normal, and his heart function was fine. The lung's re-expansion after surgery is proceeding according to the usual timetable." (Vatican News)

He added: "I believe the Pope could be fully recovered in another ten days or so." Of the visit to Latin America that Vatican News says is scheduled for 6–17 November, he said: "I would say there are no clinical reasons why this visit should not take place." (Vatican News)

Sunday 18 October: the Pope's words after the Angelus

The Press Office said, in Italian, that at noon the Holy Father appeared at the window of his study in the Apostolic Palace to pray the Angelus with the faithful and pilgrims in St Peter's Square. (Holy See Press Office)

Vatican News reported that after the Angelus the Pope thanked all those who had been praying for him, saying, "I have truly felt your support. Thank you!" He expressed "special gratitude to the doctors, nurses, and all the staff at the Gemelli Policlinic." (Vatican News)

The Pope also said: "This experience has allowed me to feel, in a particular way, a closeness to all those who are living through illness or a state of infirmity." (Vatican News)

He concluded: "Now that this delicate phase has passed—as you know—I will gradually resume my activities, always with the help of God and your prayers." (Vatican News)

Monday 19 October: recovery at the Vatican and Wednesday's General Audience (Press Office, via Vatican News)

Vatican News reported on Monday that, according to the Holy See Press Office, Pope Leo XIV is continuing his post-operative recovery in the Apostolic Palace and will hold his General Audience on Wednesday morning, 21 October, in St. Peter's Square. It added that he will remain at the Vatican this week rather than travel to his residence in Castel Gandolfo, and that the Press Office is expected to provide further updates on his other activities. At the time of our check, this update did not appear as a separate bulletin on the Press Office's own website, so we credit it as Vatican News reported it. (Vatican News)

What has not been announced

No official source cited here has given a stage for the cancer. Vatican News said on 14 October that "In the coming days, post-operation exams will be conducted on the removed tissue." No results of those exams had been announced at the time of this update (Vatican News).

CONTEXT

The Press Office issued its own statements and a statement issued by the physicians, while the Director answered journalists' questions. For 14 October we could not find the Press Office's own English text of the doctors' statement on its website, so we quote it as Vatican News published it. Where the Press Office's English text and Vatican News' rendering of the bulletin differ slightly (for example, "radical procedure" against "radical resection"), we have quoted each source exactly as it appears (Holy See Press Office; Vatican News).

OPINION (the room's view)

In this room we will stick to what has been said and leave the rest unsaid. The Holy Father spoke plainly and asked for our prayers. The fitting answer is to pray, and to wait for the next bulletin without filling the gap with guesses.
